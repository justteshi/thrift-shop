Our story · Sofia, Bulgaria

Clothes with
somewhere to go.

We believe the most interesting wardrobe is built slowly—not delivered overnight.

Thrift Shop began with a simple idea: good clothes deserve more than one life. What once felt like a secret in Bulgaria has become a confident, creative way to dress. We look beyond trends and labels for pieces that feel personal, useful, and ready to be worn again.

Why second-hand?

The antidote to
disposable fashion.

Fashion uses an enormous amount of material and energy, while wearable garments are discarded every day. Choosing pre-loved keeps existing clothes in circulation and makes room for a more individual kind of style.

We cannot solve the whole system from one shop. We can make the better choice feel exciting, accessible, and worth repeating.

From old rail to new wardrobe

Our edit,
in four steps.

  1. 01

    Source

    We search for distinctive, wearable pieces rather than buying anonymous bulk.

  2. 02

    Check

    Condition, construction, labels, and details are inspected before selection.

  3. 03

    Refresh

    Items are cleaned and prepared so they are ready for their next owner.

  4. 04

    Recirculate

    The piece returns to the world—with less waste and a completely new context.
